Sauna Timer

Quick start guide

The basic steps for timing sauna, cold bath, and rest, through to ending your session.

For details on each feature, see the Sauna Timer page.

Before you start: To record in Garmin Connect, turn FIT Save ON before starting the timer. You cannot change it after starting. Leave it OFF if you do not want an activity record.

Find your buttons

  • Red circle ENTER: Upper right (GPS)
  • Blue circle BACK: Lower right (SET)
  • Purple circle UP: Middle left (MENU)
  • Orange circle DOWN: Lower left (ABC)
  • Red circle ENTER: Upper right (START / STOP)
  • Blue circle BACK: Lower right (BACK / LAP)
  • Purple circle UP: Middle left (MENU)
  • Orange circle DOWN: Lower left (DOWN)
  • Red circle ENTER: Upper right (START / STOP)
  • Blue circle BACK: Lower right (BACK / LAP)
  • Purple circle UP: Middle left (MENU)
  • Orange circle DOWN: Lower left (DOWN)

These diagrams use representative models. Printed button names vary by model. This guide calls the buttons ENTER, BACK, UP, and DOWN.

Switch phases and finish

  1. Start

    Press ENTER on the app’s opening screen to start the sauna timer.

  2. Sauna → Cold bath → Rest

    When the timer ends and the watch vibrates, wait briefly and press ENTER to start the next phase. After rest, use the same action to start another set.

    To shorten the remaining time

    While the timer is running, press DOWN twice quickly. Sauna and rest shorten by 30 seconds; cold bath by 10 seconds. All phases can be shortened to 10 seconds remaining.

  3. Finish

    Press BACK twice quickly → select YES with UP / DOWN → ENTER. If results appear, close them with ENTER or BACK.

To switch before the timer ends

Press ENTER to open Timer Menu, select Next Timer with UP / DOWN, then press ENTER.

Save a FIT activity (optional)

If you closely track your Garmin Connect metrics

Saved FIT records appear as activities in Garmin Connect after syncing. They may affect calorie figures and, depending on your watch, metrics such as Training Effect and training load.

Keep FIT Save OFF if you want to avoid this impact. It is OFF by default. Your setting carries over to future sessions.

  1. Before starting the timer, press DOWN on the opening screen → select Setting with UP / DOWN → ENTER.
  2. Select FIT Save with UP / DOWN and press ENTER to turn it ON. Press BACK twice to return to the opening screen.
  3. Follow the basic steps and select YES when finishing. After saving, sync with Garmin Connect.

Saving requirement: At least one set must include 60 seconds or more of actual sauna timing and reach the rest phase. No record is saved if this condition is not met.
