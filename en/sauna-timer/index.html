Garmin App

Sauna Timer

An app for Garmin Instinct, Forerunner, and Fenix watches that times sauna, cold bath, and rest phases and saves a record of your session.

Read the quick start guide

Using a smartwatch in high temperatures is not recommended.
We accept no responsibility for any malfunction or damage to your smartwatch caused by its use.

Overview

Sauna Timer times sauna, cold bath, and rest phases and saves a record of each set.
One set consists of sauna, a cold bath, and rest. A series of sets makes up a session.

Download Sauna Timer (Connect IQ Store)

Use Totonel to review your saved activities on Android.
Graphs and scores help you review heart rate changes and trends across sets.

The three phases of a set

  1. Sauna
  2. Cold bath
  3. Rest

The timer counts down the duration you set for each phase: sauna, cold bath, and rest.
When the remaining time reaches zero, the watch vibrates to let you know the current phase has ended.

After the alert, the timer waits for you. Press ENTER to start the next phase.

Features

Scores for each set
At the end of each set, the app displays a TotonoiScore calculated using its own formula.
Review all sets
At the end of a session, you can review the scores for all completed sets together.
Review set details from history
In v0.6.2, saved history lets you review each set's score and maximum/minimum heart rates for sauna, cold bath, and rest.
See History for instructions.
Saved history on your watch
Save a completed session and review your ten most recent sessions on your watch.

Saving to Garmin Connect

Records saved in FIT format appear as activities in Garmin Connect.
Keep this in mind if you closely track calories burned or training load.

Garmin's calorie calculation may greatly overestimate how many calories you burned.
Keep this in mind when reviewing the figures.

A transfer failure message may appear after you save a session.
The saved activity will be transferred when you sync with Garmin Connect.

Usage precautions

Sauna Timer helps you keep track of your time in the sauna.

If you feel unwell, dizzy, short of breath, or experience strong palpitations, discontinue use immediately, regardless of the app's display or alerts.

Follow the sauna facility's rules, stay hydrated, and take enough time to rest. Do not push yourself beyond your limits.

This app is not a medical device.
The heart rate readings and scores are not intended for diagnosis, treatment, or assessing safety.

When the watch vibrates

Sauna Timer uses vibration alerts in the following situations.

At the end of a phase
The watch vibrates when the remaining time for the sauna, cold bath, or rest phase reaches zero.
30 seconds before a phase ends
When 30sec Alert is on, the watch vibrates briefly when 30 seconds remain.
When the timer remains paused
When Stop Alert is on, the watch reminds you every two minutes that the timer is still paused.
In Aufguss mode
After the elapsed time reaches 10 minutes, the watch vibrates at five-minute intervals.
When heart rate or temperature reaches a threshold
The watch provides an alert when readings are available from the device or sensors.
Alert behavior varies depending on the readings available.

Start screen

On-screen information

This screen appears when you launch the app.

  • ENTER Start the timer.
  • DOWN Open the main menu.
  • BACK Exit the app immediately if the timer has not started.

Running timer

On-screen information

  • Lime circleSet count at the top right
  • Red frameCurrent heart rate
  • Blue frameCurrent phase
  • Green frameTime remaining
  • Yellow frameCurrent time

This screen shows the current phase and remaining time as you progress through the session.

  • ENTER Open Timer Menu.
    The timer pauses while this menu is open.
  • DOWN Open the main menu.
    The timer keeps running while this menu is open.
  • UP Extend the current phase.
    Sauna and rest can be extended in one-minute steps, up to five minutes in total.
    The cold bath can be extended in 30-second steps, up to two minutes in total.
  • Press DOWN twice quickly to shorten the remaining time.
    Sauna and rest are shortened by 30 seconds each time; the cold bath is shortened by 10 seconds.
  • Press BACK twice quickly to open the exit confirmation screen while the timer keeps running.

You can shorten the remaining time to a minimum of 10 seconds for sauna, cold bath, and rest.

You cannot shorten the time while the timer is paused or waiting after a phase has ended.

Set results

On-screen information

  • Red frameSet score
  • Green frameMaximum/minimum heart rate for each phase
  • Yellow frameButton hints

When you start the next set, the app shows the previous set's TotonoiScore and the maximum and minimum heart rates for each phase.

Results are shown only for sets where the sauna timer ran for at least 60 seconds.

You return to the previous screen after 10 seconds or when you press any button.

Aufguss mode

On-screen information

  • Lime circleSet count at the top right
  • Red frameCurrent heart rate
  • Blue frameCurrent phase
  • Green frameTime elapsed since the sauna phase began
  • Yellow frameCurrent time

Aufguss mode is available only during the sauna phase.
Instead of counting down, it counts up from zero to show the time elapsed since the sauna phase began.

Press ENTER twice quickly, or select Aufguss again in Timer Menu, to return to normal mode.

  • If the set duration has not elapsed, the countdown resumes with the remaining time.
  • If the elapsed time exceeds the set duration, the countdown resumes with five seconds remaining.

For example, with a seven-minute timer, turning off Aufguss mode after six minutes resumes the countdown with one minute left. Turning it off after 10 minutes resumes the countdown with five seconds left.

After the elapsed time reaches 10 minutes, the watch vibrates at five-minute intervals.

30sec Alert
The watch vibrates briefly 30 seconds before each phase ends.

If you start the cold bath with 30 seconds or less, the watch does not vibrate for 30sec Alert.
The alert can still occur if you extend the timer to 30 seconds or more.

Stop Alert
The watch vibrates every two minutes while the timer remains paused.
Power Save
The screen updates less often when you are not using the controls.
The timer keeps counting, and alerts still work.
FIT Save
Saves the session as an activity in FIT format. This setting is off by default. Turn it on before starting a session to record a FIT activity.

You can change FIT Save only before starting the timer.
Once the timer has started, this setting cannot be changed.

Run History
On Instinct 2 / 2S / 2X, controls whether you can browse History during a session. The default is ON.
When OFF, History is hidden from Main Menu after the session starts, including while paused, in Aufguss mode, or waiting for the next phase.

History is still saved when OFF and can be viewed before starting a session. Changes made during a session apply when you return to Main Menu and are saved for the next launch.
If opening History causes the app to exit, restart it and turn this setting OFF. Other supported models do not show this setting and allow History access during a session.

Reading the summary

The first page shows the start date and time, overall TotonoiScore, set count, and the average maximum / average minimum heart rate for each phase.
The overall score appears in the circle at the top right. The set count and page number appear at the bottom.

For example, Sauna 138/111 means the maximum sauna heart rates across sets average 138 bpm, and the minimum sauna heart rates average 111 bpm.

Opening a set's details

  1. Use UP / DOWN to find the set's score page.
    Moving forward from the last page returns to the summary.
  2. Press ENTER on a page showing Enter to Detail.
    You can review the set number, score, and maximum/minimum heart rates for sauna, cold bath, and rest.
  3. Press ENTER or BACK to return to the same score page.

Set details are available for sessions newly saved with a version that supports them.
If detail data is unavailable, such as for history saved with an older version, No details appears. Missing heart rate values appear as --- and are excluded from the summary averages.

When this happens, the session is not saved to the in-app history.

Aufguss mode

On-screen information

  • Lime frame Set count at the upper left
  • Red frame Current heart rate
  • Blue frame Current phase
  • Green frame Time elapsed since the sauna phase began
  • Yellow frame Current time

Aufguss mode is available only during the sauna phase.
Instead of counting down, it counts up from zero to show the time elapsed since the sauna phase began.

Press ENTER twice quickly, or select Aufguss again in Timer Menu, to return to normal mode.

  • If the set duration has not elapsed, the countdown resumes with the remaining time.
  • If the elapsed time exceeds the set duration, the countdown resumes with five seconds remaining.

For example, with a seven-minute timer, turning off Aufguss mode after six minutes resumes the countdown with one minute left. Turning it off after 10 minutes resumes the countdown with five seconds left.

After the elapsed time reaches 10 minutes, the watch vibrates at five-minute intervals.

Reading the summary

The first page shows the start date and time, overall TotonoiScore, set count, and the average maximum / average minimum heart rate for each phase.
The overall score appears in the center. The set count and page number appear at the bottom.

For example, Sauna 138/111 means the maximum sauna heart rates across sets average 138 bpm, and the minimum sauna heart rates average 111 bpm.

Opening a set's details

  1. Use UP / DOWN to find the set's score page.
    Moving forward from the last page returns to the summary.
  2. Press ENTER on a page showing Enter to Detail.
    You can review the set number, score, and maximum/minimum heart rates for sauna, cold bath, and rest.
  3. Press ENTER or BACK to return to the same score page.

Set details are available for sessions newly saved with a version that supports them.
If detail data is unavailable, such as for history saved with an older version, No details appears. Missing heart rate values appear as --- and are excluded from the summary averages.
