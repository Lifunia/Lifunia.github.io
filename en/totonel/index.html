Android App

Totonel

Review your sauna sessions and heart rate trends with lists, graphs, and scores on Android.

The Totonel app is currently available in Japanese only.

Overview

Totonel is an Android app for reviewing activities saved with Sauna Timer.

Review heart rate changes and trends for each set.
Look back at past sessions to review your rest times and the way you plan your sets.

To use Totonel, you need an account on Intervals.icu.
Turn on FIT Save in the Sauna Timer settings.

Features

Organize sessions in a list
See dates, times, duration, location, and ratings at a glance.
Search and calendar views are also available.
Review heart rate changes
View heart rate graphs for sauna, cold bath, and rest to review each set.
Explore trends with Totonoi Score
Compare the score calculated from your heart rate with your own rating of the session.
Delete unwanted sets
Select and delete sets within a session.
The app recalculates scores using the remaining sets. Deleted sets will not reappear when you sync.

User guide

Review your records
  1. Select a session from the list on the home screen.
    In calendar view, select a date and tap that day's record.
  2. Check the rating, location, date and time, score, and heart rate in Overview.
  3. Review heart rate trends, set details, and tips for next time in Analysis and graphs.

If Hide Score until rated is on, Score is hidden until you enter a Rating.

Sync sessions
  1. To sync automatically at startup, enable Sync automatically at startup in Settings.
  2. To sync manually, swipe down on the home screen.
  3. Check the sync result in notifications or Sync status at the top of the home screen.

You must wait one minute after the previous sync before syncing again.
Open Sync status to check the last sync date and time, status, and code.

Delete sets within a session
  1. Open the session containing the sets you want to delete.
  2. Tap the Actions button at the bottom of Overview and tap Delete sets.
  3. On the Sets to delete screen, check the sets you want to remove.
    You can select several sets at once.
  4. Tap Delete to remove the selected sets and recalculate the score using the remaining sets.
    The remaining sets keep their original numbers.

After you delete sets, future syncs will not update that session.
To restore it, use Delete in the Actions menu to delete the entire session, then sync again.
The original data must still exist on Intervals.icu and fall within the sync date range.
You cannot delete sets while syncing.

Set up your Intervals API Key

Before your first sync, get your API key from Intervals.icu and enter it in the app.

Get your API key

  1. Visit Intervals.icu.
  2. Open Settings from the menu on the left.
  3. Scroll to Developer Settings near the bottom of the settings page.
  4. Click view in the API Key section.
  5. Copy the API key shown in the popup.

Keep your API key private.
Do not paste it on public pages or social media.

Enter the key in Totonel

  1. Open Settings using the gear icon at the top right of the home screen.
  2. Enter your Intervals API Key and tap Save.
  3. Tap Test connection to check that the app can connect.

Using session details

Red frameView buttons
Switch between views.

Blue frameScore switch
Switch between the standard score and Rating+.
  • Standard score: A score calculated from heart rate data.
  • Rating+: A score based on your past ratings.
    It matches the standard score until the app has learned enough from your ratings.
Cyan frameHeart rate switch
Choose whether graphs and Score use raw or corrected heart rate data.
  • Raw data: Heart rate data as imported.
  • Corrected data: Heart rate data adjusted to account for gaps and clearly implausible readings.

The selected data type appears beside the switch.

Raw data uses the imported heart rate readings without correction.
Totonel and Sauna Timer use different formulas to calculate scores, so the values may not match.

Green frameEdit button

Orange frameScreenshot button

Pink frameActions button
Recalculate the score, exclude the session from Rating+ learning, or delete session data or sets.
  • Recalculate: Recalculate the score after changing the Rating.
  • Exclude from Rating+ learning: Exclude this session from Rating+ learning.

Set details

Set Score
A score calculated from the metrics for that set.
Depth
Shows how far your heart rate drops during rest after rising in the sauna.
A larger drop produces a higher score.
Speed
Shows how quickly your heart rate drops after you begin resting.
A faster drop produces a higher score.
Stability
Measures how stable your heart rate is during rest.
A steadier heart rate and enough time to rest produce a higher score.

Usage notes

Notes on reviewing your records

Totonel is an app for recording and reviewing sessions.
Heart rate readings and scores are not intended for diagnosis, treatment, or assessing safety.
If you have concerns about your physical condition, discontinue use regardless of what the app shows.

Follow the sauna facility's rules, stay hydrated, and take enough time to rest.
